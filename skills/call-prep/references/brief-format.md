# Brief format

Target length: under 250 words. A rep reads this on a phone, walking to a desk.
Sections with nothing real in them are omitted, not padded.

## Shape

```
**{Company} — {call type}, {when}**
{One line: who you are talking to, and why this meeting exists.}

**Where they are**
{2-3 sentences from the researcher's snapshot. Facts only.}

**Why now**
- {Buying trigger, with its source domain in parentheses}
- {Second trigger, if one exists}

**Our history with them**
- {CRM: contact exists since X / no prior record}
- {RevCrew: sequence sent DATE, step 2 opened, replied DATE with an objection about pricing}

**Lead with**
- {Talking point tied to a specific trigger, not a generic value prop}
- {Second talking point}

**Ask**
1. {Question}
2. {Question}
3. {Question}

**Not established**
{The researcher's gaps, verbatim. Delete the section only if there are none.}
```

## Worked example

```
**Northwind Logistics — discovery, 2pm**
Priya Raman, VP Revenue Operations. Inbound from the pricing page last Thursday.

**Where they are**
Freight visibility software for mid-market shippers, roughly 90 employees.
Sells to logistics managers through a direct field team. Publishes an
engineering blog with recent posts on warehouse telemetry.

**Why now**
- Series B in April, led by an infrastructure fund (techcrunch.com)
- Four open roles on the revenue team, two of them RevOps (northwind.com/careers)

**Our history with them**
- No HubSpot contact for priya@northwind.com — this is a first conversation
- RevCrew has not sent this account anything

**Lead with**
- Two RevOps hires open at once usually means reporting is the bottleneck, not headcount
- Their engineering blog suggests the telemetry data exists but is not reaching the revenue team

**Ask**
1. What made you look at this now rather than six months ago?
2. Who else feels the problem you are describing?
3. What happens if you do nothing this quarter?

**Not established**
No pricing or packaging information found. Current tooling unknown — no
job posts or blog mention a CRM or a BI vendor.
```

## Notes on tone

- Write for someone who will paraphrase this out loud. No jargon they would not say.
- Attribute claims to the source domain in parentheses, never a bare URL.
- "Our history with them" is the section reps actually need and most briefs skip.
  Cold and warm are different conversations, and getting it wrong is obvious to the buyer.

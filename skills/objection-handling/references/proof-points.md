# Proof points

**This file ships empty on purpose.** It is the one place in the skill that
belongs to you rather than to RevCrew, in the same way `app/icp.yaml` owns the
rubric. Replace every `TODO` with something true about your business.

Until a section is filled in, the copilot is instructed not to cite it. A
missing proof point produces a response without one, which is recoverable. An
invented customer name is not.
